**Description:**

## WeatherMaps: Immersion

When the weather turns where you stand, your **minimap and world map get caught in it**. A haze closes in around your position, and flakes, rain streaks or wind-blown dust drift across the map, so roads and terrain get harder to read the way they do when you are actually in the storm.

### Features
- **Snow, rain and sandstorm**, each with its own particles and haze colour, driven by the game's real weather. No fake timers.
- **Heavier storm, tighter haze.** The clear area around your arrow shrinks, drifts and breathes, so visibility feels alive.
- **Cloud layer and soft puffs** keep the haze from looking like a flat colour.
- **Minimap and world map.** The minimap overlay sits under the compass ring. The world map overlay sits under pins and controls and only shows on the zone you are standing in.
- **Hides indoors** and when the weather is clear. Never blocks clicks or tooltips.
- **Everything is adjustable** with `/weathermaps`: overall strength, cloud, puffs, particle density, speed.

### Try it without waiting for a storm
`/weathermaps preview snow`, `preview rain`, `preview sand` (optional intensity 0 to 1), then `/weathermaps preview off`.

### Notes
- Built for **WoW Forever** (client 1.60.x).
- Fully restart the game the first time you install it, because new texture files are not loaded by `/reload`.
- Beta: snow and rain were checked against real storms. Sandstorm was seen once, briefly. Please report anything odd.
